# Data Protection Act 2018 (Republic of Veloria)

Fictional statute written for evaluation. It is not law anywhere.

## Part 1. Application

1.1 This Act applies to every controller that processes the personal data of more than 500 data subjects in Veloria in any calendar year.

1.2 A controller that processes the personal data of more than 5,000 data subjects must appoint a data protection officer.

## Part 2. Breach notification

2.1 As enacted in 2018, a controller must notify the Data Commissioner of a personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

2.2 The Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2022 replaced the notification period in section 2.1. From 1 July 2022, a controller must notify the Data Commissioner of a personal data breach within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

2.3 A notification must describe the nature of the breach, the categories of data subjects affected and the measures taken.

## Part 3. Penalties

3.1 As enacted in 2018, the Data Commissioner may impose a penalty of up to 2% of a controller's annual turnover.

3.2 From 1 July 2022, the Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2022 raised the maximum penalty to 4% of a controller's annual turnover.

3.3 The Data Commissioner must give written reasons for every penalty.
