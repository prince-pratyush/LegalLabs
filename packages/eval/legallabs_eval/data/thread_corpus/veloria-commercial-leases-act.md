# Commercial Leases Act 2015 (Republic of Veloria)

Fictional statute written for evaluation. It is not law anywhere.

## Part 1. Rent review

1.1 The rent under a commercial lease in Veloria may be reviewed no more often than every 3 years.

1.2 A landlord must give the tenant at least 60 days' written notice of a rent review.

## Part 2. Security bonds

2.1 As enacted in 2015, a landlord could require a security bond of up to 6 months' rent.

2.2 The Commercial Leases (Bonds) Amendment Act 2020 reduced the cap. From 1 January 2020, a landlord may require a security bond of no more than 3 months' rent.

2.3 A bond must be held in a separate trust account and returned within 30 days after the lease ends.
