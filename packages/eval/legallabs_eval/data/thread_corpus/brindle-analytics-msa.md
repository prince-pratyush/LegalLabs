# Master Services Agreement: Brindle Analytics and Corvane Retail

Fictional contract written for evaluation. Both companies are invented.

## 1. Term

1.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 36 months.

1.2 After the initial term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives at least 90 days' written notice of non-renewal.

## 2. Fees

2.1 Corvane Retail must pay each invoice within 45 days of receipt.

## 3. Liability

3.1 Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim arose.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party must keep the other's confidential information secret for 5 years after this Agreement ends.

## 5. Governing law

5.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Veloria.
