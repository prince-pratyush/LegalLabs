# Supply Agreement: Harbourline Components and Ostrava Marine

Fictional contract written for evaluation. Both companies are invented.

## 1. Orders

1.1 Ostrava Marine must order at least 2,000 units in each contract year.

## 2. Price

2.1 Prices may be adjusted once a year in line with inflation, but no adjustment may exceed 3% in any year.

## 3. Delivery

3.1 Harbourline Components delivers DDP Port Selwyn.

## 4. Warranty

4.1 Harbourline Components warrants each unit against defects for 24 months from delivery.

## 5. Termination

5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving 180 days' written notice.
