# Okafor Imports v Linden Shipping [2023] VCA 27

Fictional judgment written for evaluation. The parties and court do not exist.

Court of Appeal of Veloria. Judgment delivered on 2 October 2023.

## Holding

1. The Court of Appeal approved the rule in Marlow Textiles v Tessaly Freight Lines but narrowed it.

2. From this judgment, a party relying on gross negligence to escape a limitation clause must plead the gross negligence with full particulars.

3. Because Okafor Imports had not pleaded particulars of gross negligence, the limitation clause applied and its claim was limited to 20,000 crowns.
