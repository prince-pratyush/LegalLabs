# Marlow Textiles v Tessaly Freight Lines [2019] VHC 114

Fictional judgment written for evaluation. The parties and court do not exist.

High Court of Veloria. Judgment delivered on 14 March 2019 by Justice Amara Quill.

## Facts

1. Tessaly Freight Lines carried a consignment of dyed fabric for Marlow Textiles. The containers were left unrefrigerated on the quay for nine days and the fabric was ruined.

2. The carriage contract contained a limitation clause capping the carrier's liability at 5,000 crowns per container.

## Holding

3. The court held that a limitation clause cannot exclude or limit liability for gross negligence unless the clause says so in express words.

4. Leaving the containers unrefrigerated for nine days was gross negligence, so the cap did not apply.

5. The court awarded Marlow Textiles damages of 412,000 crowns.
