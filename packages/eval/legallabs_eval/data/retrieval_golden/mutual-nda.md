# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This agreement is made between Brightwater Analytics Pty Ltd (ACN 000 000 001) and Kestrel Logistics Limited (each a "Party"). The parties wish to explore a possible route-optimisation partnership (the "Purpose") and will share information for that Purpose only.

## 1. Confidential Information

1.1 "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one Party (the "Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient"), whether oral, written or electronic, including pricing, customer lists, source code, shipment volumes and business plans, whether or not it is marked as confidential.

1.2 Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public other than through a breach of this agreement; (b) the Recipient already lawfully held without restriction before disclosure; (c) the Recipient independently develops without use of the Discloser's information; or (d) a third party lawfully gives the Recipient without a duty of confidence.

## 2. Obligations of the Recipient

2.1 The Recipient must keep the Confidential Information secret and use it only for the Purpose.

2.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, officers and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose, provided each of them is bound by obligations of confidence no less protective than this agreement. The Recipient is liable for any breach by those persons.

2.3 The Recipient must not reverse engineer, decompile or analyse the composition of any software, sample or prototype supplied by the Discloser.

## 3. Compelled Disclosure

If the Recipient is required by law, a court order or the rules of a stock exchange to disclose Confidential Information, it must, where lawful, give the Discloser prompt written notice so that the Discloser can seek a protective order, and must disclose only the part that is legally required.

## 4. Return and Destruction

Within 14 days after a written request from the Discloser, or after this agreement ends, the Recipient must return or destroy all documents containing Confidential Information and certify in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may keep one archival copy where required by law, which remains subject to this agreement.

## 5. Term

This agreement starts on the date of the last signature and continues for two years. The obligations of confidence in clauses 1 to 4 survive for three years after the agreement ends.

## 6. No Licence

Nothing in this agreement grants the Recipient any licence or other right in the Discloser's intellectual property, other than the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

## 7. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of this agreement and that the Discloser may seek injunctive relief or specific performance in addition to any other remedy.

## 8. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia, and the Parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of New South Wales.
