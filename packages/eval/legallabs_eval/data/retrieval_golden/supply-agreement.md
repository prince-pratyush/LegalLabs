# Supply Agreement

This agreement is between Ironbark Components Ltd (the "Supplier") and Tidewater Marine Pty Ltd (the "Buyer") for the supply of stainless steel fittings (the "Goods").

## 1. Orders and Delivery

1.1 The Buyer may place purchase orders for Goods from time to time. An order is binding only once the Supplier accepts it in writing.

1.2 The Supplier must deliver the Goods DAP to the Buyer's warehouse in Fremantle (Incoterms 2020) on the delivery date stated in the accepted order. Time of delivery is of the essence.

1.3 If the Supplier delivers late, it must pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the price of the late Goods for each day of delay, up to 10% of that price.

## 2. Risk and Title

Risk in the Goods passes to the Buyer on delivery. Title passes to the Buyer when the Supplier has received payment in full for those Goods.

## 3. Price and Payment

The prices are fixed for the first 12 months and then may be adjusted once a year by reference to the published steel price index. The Buyer must pay within 45 days after the end of the month in which it receives a correct tax invoice.

## 4. Warranties

4.1 The Supplier warrants that the Goods will conform to the specifications, be of merchantable quality and be free from defects in materials and workmanship for 18 months after delivery (the "Warranty Period").

4.2 If the Buyer notifies a defect during the Warranty Period, the Supplier must, at its option and cost, repair or replace the defective Goods within 20 business days.

## 5. Indemnity and Insurance

5.1 The Supplier indemnifies the Buyer against all loss, damage and costs arising from any claim that the Goods infringe a third party's intellectual property rights, or from death or personal injury caused by defective Goods.

5.2 The Supplier must maintain product liability insurance of at least AUD 20 million per occurrence for the term of this agreement and for three years afterwards.

## 6. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for a failure or delay caused by an event beyond its reasonable control, including natural disaster, war, epidemic or industrial action not limited to that party's workforce. The affected party must notify the other promptly and use reasonable efforts to mitigate. If the event continues for more than 60 days, either party may terminate the affected orders by notice.

## 7. Exclusivity

During the term the Buyer must buy all of its requirements for the Goods in Western Australia from the Supplier, and the Supplier must not supply equivalent goods to any competitor of the Buyer in Western Australia.

## 8. Term and Governing Law

This agreement runs for three years from the start date. It is governed by the laws of Western Australia.
