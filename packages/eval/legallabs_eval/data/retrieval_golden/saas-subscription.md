# Software Subscription Agreement

This agreement is between Lumen Ledger Inc. (the "Provider") and Harbourside Foods Pty Ltd (the "Customer") for access to the Provider's hosted accounting platform (the "Service").

## 1. Subscription and Fees

1.1 The Customer subscribes to the Service for the number of user seats stated in the order form.

1.2 The Provider will invoice the subscription fees annually in advance. The Customer must pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of the invoice date. Overdue amounts accrue interest at 1% per month until paid.

1.3 The Provider may increase the fees for any renewal term by giving at least 60 days' written notice before the renewal date; any increase is capped at 5% per year.

## 2. Service Levels

2.1 The Provider will make the Service available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least 48 hours in advance.

2.2 If availability falls below that level, the Customer is entitled to a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee for each full 0.5% shortfall, up to a maximum of 25% of the monthly fee. Service credits are the Customer's sole remedy for unavailability.

## 3. Customer Data

3.1 The Customer owns all data it uploads to the Service ("Customer Data"). The Provider may process Customer Data only to provide the Service and in accordance with the Customer's documented instructions.

3.2 The Provider must notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a breach of security leading to unauthorised access to Customer Data.

3.3 Customer Data will be hosted in Australia and will not be transferred outside Australia without the Customer's prior written consent.

## 4. Limitation of Liability

4.1 Neither party is liable for any indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit, revenue or goodwill.

4.2 Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim.

4.3 The limits in this clause do not apply to a party's liability for breach of clause 3 (Customer Data), fraud, or personal injury.

## 5. Term and Termination

5.1 The initial term is 12 months from the start date. The agreement then renews automatically for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the current term.

5.2 The Customer may terminate for convenience by giving 60 days' written notice, but prepaid fees are not refundable.

5.3 Either party may terminate immediately by notice if the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days after receiving notice of it, or becomes insolvent.

5.4 On termination the Provider will make Customer Data available for export for 30 days and then delete it.

## 6. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.
