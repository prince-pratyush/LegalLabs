# Employment Contract

This contract is between Quill & Compass Consulting Pty Ltd (the "Employer") and Jordan Example (the "Employee") for the position of Senior Data Engineer.

## 1. Commencement and Probation

The employment starts on 1 March and is ongoing. The first six months are a probationary period, during which either party may end the employment by giving one week's written notice.

## 2. Remuneration

The Employee will be paid a base salary of AUD 165,000 per year plus superannuation at the statutory rate, paid fortnightly in arrears. The salary will be reviewed each July but need not be increased.

## 3. Hours and Location

The ordinary hours are 38 per week, Monday to Friday, plus reasonable additional hours. The Employee may work from home up to three days per week, subject to business needs.

## 4. Leave

The Employee is entitled to four weeks' paid annual leave, ten days' paid personal or carer's leave per year, and long service leave under the applicable state legislation.

## 5. Intellectual Property

All intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of the employment, or using the Employer's resources, belongs to the Employer on creation. The Employee assigns any such rights to the Employer and consents to any infringement of moral rights reasonably required for the Employer to use the work.

## 6. Confidentiality

The Employee must not, during or after the employment, use or disclose any confidential information of the Employer or its clients except as required to perform their duties or by law.

## 7. Termination

7.1 After probation, either party may end the employment by giving four weeks' written notice, or five weeks if the Employee is over 45 years old and has at least two years' service. The Employer may pay in lieu of notice.

7.2 The Employer may end the employment without notice for serious misconduct.

## 8. Post-Employment Restraints

For 12 months after the employment ends, the Employee must not solicit or accept work from any client of the Employer with whom the Employee dealt in the last 12 months of employment, and must not induce any employee of the Employer to leave. Each restraint is a separate and severable obligation.

## 9. Governing Law

This contract is governed by the laws of Queensland, Australia.
